Transfers secondary unit of measure information from Manufacturing Orders to
Stock Moves, ensuring that both the produced product and consumed components
maintain their secondary unit of measure information in stock movements.

**Table of contents**

.. contents::
   :local:

Configuration
=============

1. Install the module ``mrp_production_secondary_unit`` (this module depends on it).
2. Configure secondary units of measure in your Manufacturing Orders.
3. When Manufacturing Orders are confirmed and stock moves are created, the
   secondary unit information will be automatically transferred.

Usage
=====

This module works automatically:

* When a Manufacturing Order is confirmed, stock moves are created
* The secondary unit of measure from the Manufacturing Order is transferred
  to the finished product stock moves
* For raw materials, the secondary unit is taken from the product configuration
* All stock moves related to the Manufacturing Order will have the secondary
  unit information available
